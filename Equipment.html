Before you mobilize

  • Confirm utility locate status and permit requirements for the day.
  • Match crew assignments to qualified operators and spotters.
  • Verify the correct manuals and quick-reference sheets are in each truck.
  • Document any existing damage before equipment startup.

Field quality checks

  • Use a pre-task brief to align on hazards, access points, and shutdown plans.
  • Log locator readings and mark confirmations before excavation begins.
  • Record defects immediately and tag tools out of service when needed.
  • Close each shift with cleanup, fuel/charge checks, and maintenance notes.
