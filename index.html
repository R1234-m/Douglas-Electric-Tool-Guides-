          Field tips

            How to use it in the field

            • 1. Charge the battery and confirm the locator powers on normally.
            • 2. Inspect the cable, antenna, and connectors before leaving the truck.
            • 3. Verify the work zone, utility locate marks, and safe access points.
            • 4. Sweep the area slowly and confirm the signal before marking the utility.
            • 5. If the signal weakens or becomes unstable, stop and check the setup before proceeding.

            Quick compliance check

            Michigan electrical jobsite start checklist

            Use this 60-second check before crews start work each day.

            Before work begins

            • 811 ticket is active and utility marks are verified onsite.
            • Assigned crew includes qualified personnel for the planned tasks.
            • Daily hazard brief is completed with stop-work triggers reviewed.
            • Tools, cords, and GFCI protection are inspected and documented.

            During active work

            • Use de-energized workflow whenever feasible and verify before touch.
            • Maintain traffic control and barricade limits around the work area.
            • Log locator checks, field changes, and near misses in real time.
            • Pause and re-brief immediately if site conditions change.
