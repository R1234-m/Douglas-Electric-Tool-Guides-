Crew development

Training resources for field teams

Use this page to structure onboarding, refreshers, and documented competency checks for electrical field operations in Michigan.

New-hire first week plan

  • Day 1: PPE, stop-work authority, and communication standards.
  • Day 2: Utility locate workflow and mark recognition review.
  • Day 3: Tool startup and shutdown drills with supervisor signoff.
  • Day 4-5: Ride-along with performance notes and final check.

Monthly refresher topics

  • Voltage testing workflow and test-before-touch sequence.
  • Lockout/tagout verification steps for service work.
  • Generator load management and temporary power safety.
  • Near-miss review and corrective action follow-through.

Toolbox talk rotation

  • Week 1: Excavation tolerance zones and 811 ticket review.
  • Week 2: Ground-fault protection and extension cord inspection.
  • Week 3: Traffic control around active work zones.
  • Week 4: Heat/cold stress and hydration planning.

Documentation to keep current

  • Qualified worker list and supervised trainee assignments.
  • Daily briefing sign-in and hazard analysis records.
  • Equipment inspection logs and out-of-service tags.
  • Incident, near-miss, and corrective-action reports.

Michigan-focused training reminders

  • Non-union teams must still follow MIOSHA/OSHA rules in full.
  • Only properly licensed or supervised personnel perform electrical work.
  • Confirm permit, utility locate, and municipal requirements before work.
  • Re-brief crews whenever scope or site conditions change.

Implementation tip

Keep training practical and verifiable

Pair each lesson with a field demonstration, then record who completed it, when it was taught, and who signed off. This keeps training useful during audits and day-to-day operations.
