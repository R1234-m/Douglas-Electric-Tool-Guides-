Safety first

Safety center

Protect people, property, and the jobsite with clear checklists and daily reminders.

PPE essentials

  • High visibility vest
  • Hard hat and safety glasses
  • Work gloves and steel-toe boots
  • Hearing protection and face shield when needed

Daily checklist

  • Inspect all equipment before use
  • Verify batteries are charged
  • Check cables and connectors
  • Review work zone controls and traffic plan

Emergency response

  • Call 911 for emergencies
  • Stop work if conditions become unsafe
  • Report all incidents and near misses
  • Follow utility locate and permit requirements
