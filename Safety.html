Safety Center

Safety is the responsibility of every Douglas Electric employee. Always inspect equipment before use and never bypass safety procedures.

Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

🦺 High Visibility Vest
👷 Hard Hat
🥽 Safety Glasses
🧤 Work Gloves
👢 Steel Toe Boots
🎧 Hearing Protection

Daily Safety Checklist

  • Inspect all equipment.
  • Verify batteries are charged.
  • Check cables for damage.
  • Inspect vehicle and trailer.
  • Confirm PPE is worn.
  • Review the work plan.
  • Call for utility locates when required.
  • Establish a safe work zone.

General Safety Rules

  • Never excavate without verifying utility markings.
  • Keep unauthorized personnel away from the work area.
  • Stop work immediately if unsafe conditions are found.
  • Report damaged equipment immediately.
  • Follow OSHA, state, and company safety requirements.
  • Never operate unfamiliar equipment without training.

Incident Reporting

Immediately report all injuries, equipment damage, near misses, and utility strikes to your supervisor.
